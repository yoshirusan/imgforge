Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

The short version

There are no accounts, no passwords, and no email required. We store a random session identifier in a cookie, the prompts you type, the images you generate, and your IP address for rate limiting and abuse prevention. Advertisements may set their own cookies.

What we store

DataWhyKept for
A random session ID in a cookie To keep your credit balance attached to your browser 30 days, or until you clear cookies
Your prompts To generate the image, and to show your gallery Until the gallery entry is deleted
Generated images To display and download them Until storage rotation
IP address Rate limiting and abuse prevention Rolling hourly window for limits; retained in logs
Credit grant events To grant credits and prevent repeated abuse Until account deletion or cleanup

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no passwords, no email collection.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not train models on your prompts.
  • We do not use tracking pixels or analytics beyond what is described here.

Cookies

We set one first-party cookie, imgforge_sid, which holds a random identifier. It keeps your credits attached to your browser. It contains no personal information.

Third-party cookies: when advertising is enabled, ad networks may set their own cookies or identifiers to measure and personalise ads. Those are controlled by the network, not by us. Where required by law (for example in the EU and UK), you will be shown a consent prompt before non-essential cookies are set, and you can refuse.

Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your data, or to object to processing. Because there is no account, the practical way to remove your data is to clear your cookies (which detaches your session) and to contact us at [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL] with the image URL if you want a specific image removed.

Children

This service is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Where processing happens

Image generation runs on a privately operated machine; requests are routed to it through a tunnel service. The web pages themselves are served by GitHub Pages. Advertisements, when enabled, are served by the ad network you see in the consent prompt.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will be noted on the main page.
